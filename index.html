What Ledger does

Everything expires.
Nothing should catch you off guard.

📅

Expiry tracking

Bold countdown timers show exactly how many days until each document expires. Sorted by urgency across your whole family.

📷

Smart scanning

Point your camera and Ledger reads the document automatically — expiry dates, document numbers, issuing authorities. 100% on-device.

🔔

Renewal reminders

Alerts at 90, 30, and 7 days before expiry. Tap a notification to jump straight to the document.

👨‍👩‍👧

Built for families

Separate profiles for every family member. Filter by person to see only their documents. One vault for the whole household.

📱

Home screen widget

See your next upcoming renewals without opening the app. Small and medium widget sizes for your home screen.

📤

Export anytime

Export your full vault as PDFs organized by family member and category. Your documents are always portable.

Privacy

No servers. No accounts.
No one but you.

Ledger has no backend. Your document scans and data are stored only on your device and in your personal iCloud private database — the same place Apple stores your Health data and Keychain. We cannot see your documents. Nobody can.
